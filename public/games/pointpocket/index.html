About PointPocket

An offer-wall style rewards app centered on quick tasks and playable ads. Completed offers contribute to a balance that can be redeemed for gift cards under the app's terms. Developed by Pocket Labs, it's currently on version 1.0.9 and available on Android.

PointPocket follows the core loop typical of the offer wall genre. Players take part in regular gameplay and events; activity can contribute toward gift cards distributed by Pocket Labs under its own program rules.
